When a payment rail becomes a weapon, someone starts looking for a rail that can't be switched off.

The dollar, SWIFT, and correspondent banking were built as infrastructure. They are increasingly deployed as coercion. Every time that happens, the demand for neutral settlement grows.

What institutions are circling around, without naming it, is neutral ground. Settlement infrastructure with no national preference. No sanctions jurisdiction. No political override. A clearing layer that works the same regardless of who is currently unhappy with whom.

Bitcoin was built to solve a different problem: the trusted third party. It was built with this property by design. Permissionless. Borderless. No counterparty in the clearing chain. No one removes you for political reasons because no one runs it.

The argument for neutral money took fifteen years to make. The world is now building the evidence that makes it obvious.
