The deck is a performance.

The signal lives in the questions they didn't prepare for. The hesitation. The redirect. The answer that runs long because they're working it out as they speak.

Twenty years on both sides of this. Regulatory audits, compliance reviews, vendor evaluations. The prepared answers are almost always clean. The unscripted ones are almost always true.

One recent investment: the founding team sent eleven prepared Q&As before the call. Professional. Organized. Told me nothing.

Then forty-five minutes of questions not on the list. That call told me everything.

Ask the question they didn't prepare for. Then listen.
