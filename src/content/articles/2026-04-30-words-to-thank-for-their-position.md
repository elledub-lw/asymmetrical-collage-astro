I rarely pay attention to lyrics in a song *(something my wife does not understand)* as I care more about the riffs, the sound, the texture. I believe Stewart Copeland and Sting had the same divergence. I'm clearly team Copeland (one of my all time favourite drummers), but you can't deny how good Sting's lyrics are in **De Do Do Do, De Da Da Da**:

*"Poets, priests and politicians*
*Have words to thank for their positions*
*Words that scream for your submission*
*And no one's jamming their transmission*

*And when their eloquence escapes you*
*Their logic ties you up and rapes you"*

...and it's a fabulous song :)
